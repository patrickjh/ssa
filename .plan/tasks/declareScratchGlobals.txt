declareScratchGlobals — name or declare scratch variables

Why:
POSIX sh has no portable locals. Names such as REPLY persist in the
shell and can be reused by accident. listOfIssues also mentioned
API_ERROR, HEADER_VALUE, SED_EXTRACT_BETWEEN_FENCES; some of those
may already be gone — grep before renaming.

Do:
Declare leftovers in the Internal block, or use more explicit names.
Do not shadow top-level variables.

Size:
About 1 line to change in `ssa` (1–3): declare REPLY in Internal, or
read into an already-named path and drop REPLY. Tests and AGENTS.md
are extra, not in that count.
