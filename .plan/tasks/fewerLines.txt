fewerLines — inline thin helpers; fewer lines in ssa

Why:
ssa is one file with an 1000-line cap. Many helpers exist for a
single call site and a one-test body (agent_is_done,
cleanup_temp_folder). handle_model_response
repeats return $LOOP_AGAIN on every arm. That is extra lines, not
extra clarity. No behavior change.

Do:
Shrink product ssa. Stay under 80 columns. Keep call-order layout.

- One-call-site function whose body is one [ ] test and a return:
  inline at the caller (Guard + return). Delete the function.
- handle_model_response: elif chain, one action per arm, one
  return $LOOP_AGAIN at the end. Keep # complete as TASK_DONE.
- Guard + return one-liners where the then is only return
  (already Style).
- Inline other one-call wrappers that are a single command
  (append_assistant_turn is append_json_message). Keep the name
  when it is the documentation (if reply_is_blank; then in the
  dispatch).

Do not inline first_action_line, die,
create_file_no_overwrite, or other helpers with several callers
or a real job. Do not add write_or_die. Do not split ssa into
a second file. Do not delete comments. Do not unwrap wrapped
lines here; that is unnecessaryLineBreaks.txt.

Land after shortFunctions.txt. That job splits long bodies; this
job cuts total lines. Do not re-split the elif dispatch to meet
a 15-line body cap. unnecessaryVariables.txt is strings, not
functions. unnecessaryLineBreaks.txt joins wraps that fit in 80.

Related: shortFunctions.txt. unnecessaryVariables.txt.
unnecessaryLineBreaks.txt.

About 30–60 lines out of ssa. Tests extra only if a string
changes. Stay under 1000.
