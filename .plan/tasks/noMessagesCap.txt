noMessagesCap — drop MAX_MESSAGES_BYTES; let the provider reject

Why:
ssa caps messages.json at 131072 bytes. That is not the model's
context window. Silent drop of old turns is the real bug.
Dying at 128KB is a second, arbitrary limit. A long run that
still fits the provider should go through. One that does not
should fail at curl or jq.

Think first. Do not implement this until a human decides.
If the local cap stays, do messagesSlidingWindow instead
(fail closed, no drops). If the cap goes, that die-at-cap
job may not be needed.
