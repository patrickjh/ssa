dropJqBinaryFlag — consider dropping JQ_BINARY_FLAG

Why:
Every jq call threads unquoted $JQ_BINARY_FLAG so some builds
do not write CRLF. setup_jq_binary_flag probes jq -b once
because jq 1.6 and older lack it. ssa is POSIX-only (Windows
use WSL). Unix jq already writes LF. The probe, the internal
name, and the unquoted expansion exist for a Windows jq
behavior the product does not run.

Do:
Decide first. Preferred: drop -b. Remove JQ_BINARY_FLAG,
setup_jq_binary_flag, and the startup probe. Call jq with
no extra flag. Write/edit still need exact bytes; on POSIX
jq -j already does that. Do not require jq 1.7+ only to
keep -b. Do not wrap jq in a helper just to hide the flag.

If no: keep the probe and the flag. Do not always pass -b
(that dies on jq 1.6). Do not document a Windows jq
workaround; the product still says use WSL.

If yes: drop the AGENTS.md sentences about jq -b and the
startup probe (write-request bytes and model-reply extract).
Do not add a jq version check.

Size:
If no: 0. If yes: about 8 lines removed from ssa (5–12):
the name, the setup function, the call. jq lines stay;
they lose $JQ_BINARY_FLAG. AGENTS.md is extra.

Tests:
If no: none. If yes: write/edit stories still keep trailing
blank lines and exact payload bytes. Fake curl, SSA_NO_ASK=1.
No new story for -b.
