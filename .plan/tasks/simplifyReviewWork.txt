simplifyReviewWork — fewer lines for the post-run review

Why:
experiments/ssaWithReview already always reviews after the loop.
The extra POST is wrapped in fail-soft, a copy-and-swap of
messages.json, a review/ folder, and feedback.txt. Those rules
are not required for a useful review and cost most of the new
lines.

Do:
Slim review_work in experiments/ssaWithReview. Do not grow product
ssa to match the thick fork. Prefer reuse over a parallel HTTP
path.

Drop fail-soft: put die back in send_curl_json_request and
save_model_reply_to_file (same as product ssa), and call call_curl
from review. A failed review curl is then a harness failure.

Drop the jq copy-and-swap of the system turn. Append a user turn
with append_json_message (plain-text ask: what would make the next
run better). Keep-temp messages.json may include that ask.

Drop review/ and feedback.txt. Increment PROMPT_COUNTER, call
setup_per_prompt_log_folder, then call_curl. Logs land in the next
promptN/. Print from latestModelResponse.txt.

Do not run handle_model_response, ask, or the sandbox. Do not
append the review reply as a chat turn. Still print
"Feedback from model:" on stderr, then the reply, then a newline,
then the existing status line. Still skip review on die and
SIGINT/SIGTERM. Always on: no flag.

Keep -h to one short clause. Product tests stay on ../ssa.

About −15 lines in experiments/ssaWithReview (net toward ~10–15
above product ssa). review_work shrinks; call_curl / send / save
go back to the product shape. No new flag.

None in tests/ until this is product. Smoke: --no-ask --keep-temp,
stderr has Feedback from model: then the status line, extra curl
is promptN+1, stdout is still script output only.
