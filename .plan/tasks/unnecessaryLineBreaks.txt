unnecessaryLineBreaks — join wraps that already fit in 80

Why:
ssa is one file with a 1000-line cap. Many `|| die`, `if`/`then`,
and continued strings are two lines when one would still be
≤80 characters. That is extra wraps, not extra clarity.

Do:
In product `ssa` only, join a wrapped line onto the previous
line when the result stays ≤80 characters. Typical cases:
`cmd || die "…"`, `[ ] || die`, `if …; then …; fi` on one
line when Style already allows it, and a continued quoted
string that fits. Keep names; do not shorten to fit.
Do not join if the one-liner would exceed 80. Do not strip
comments. Do not change behavior.

Land after fewerLines.txt. That job inlines helpers and
must not unwrap; this job is the unwrap pass.

Related: fewerLines.txt. unnecessaryVariables.txt.
AGENTS.md line length (80 columns).

Size:
A cut in `ssa` (one line per successful join). Tests extra
only if a string on stderr/stdout changes. Stay under 1000.

Tests:
sh tests/runTests.sh. Fake curl, SSA_NO_ASK=1. No behavior
change.
