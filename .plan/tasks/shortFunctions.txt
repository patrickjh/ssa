shortFunctions — keep function bodies short

Why:
Most helpers in ssa are already small. Four are over 15 lines
(count from `name() {` through the closing `}`):

  handle_model_response     30  reply-kind dispatch
  setup_temp_files          20  path list plus create
  build_curl_json_request   20  extra JSON plus jq merge
  apply_unique_replace      17  jq apply, check .ok, write bytes

send_curl_json_request is 15 already. Later jobs
(mixedReplyKinds, reviewWork) grow the dispatch, so split it
before they land if this runs.

Do:
Split until every function is 15 lines or fewer. Extract helpers
that have one job and a long, explicit name. Keep call-order
layout. No behavior change.

Leave list functions as lists. setup_temp_files and
check_required_commands grow when a path or tool is added, not
when the job gets harder. A second function is fine when it is
a real job (create the files after the paths are bound). Do not
loop over names just to shrink a list. Do not split into part1 /
part2.

Do not delete comments or un-wrap 80-column lines to fit the
count. values.txt is the ranking if it has landed; do not argue
from a numeric cap against Understandable.

Ask before adding it to Style. It may stay a one-shot reshape,
or it may become a standing rule next to the 80-character line
cap (same 15-line count, same list-function exception). Do not
copy the ranking into -h or README.

Size:
About 0 net in `ssa` (20–40 lines touched, 2–4 new functions).
AGENTS.md Style is extra if the rule lands. No behavior change.
Run sh tests/runTests.sh.
